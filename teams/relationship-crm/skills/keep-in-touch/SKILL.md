# Keep in Touch

## Process

1. Find important contacts the user has not spoken to in a while.
2. Look for a specific reason now: a job change, launch, shared interest, or promise owed.
3. Pick three to seven people and explain each choice.
4. Draft a short, personal note for each.

## Guardrails

Never invent shared history. Drafts only.
