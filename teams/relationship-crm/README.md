# Relationship CRM

A two-bot relationship desk. Archivist builds and maintains a personal CRM from your email and calendar history: who you know, how you met, when you last spoke, and what you talked about. Connector reviews it weekly and suggests a few people to reach out to, with a specific reason and a draft note. Nothing is sent without approval.

## Members

- **Archivist, Relationship Archivist.** Owns the relationship database.
- **Connector, Relationship Manager.** Owns weekly nudges and draft notes.

## Included skills

- **Relationship Index.** Build and update one record per real person from email and calendar history.
- **Keep in Touch.** Suggest a few timely check-ins with a reason and a draft.

Needs Google Calendar, Gmail. Optional: Notion. Connections happen inside Tazmu. Nothing is sent, published, or changed without your approval.
