# Escalation Pack

## Process

1. Collect the account, plan, payment history, and prior tickets.
2. For bugs, write steps to reproduce, expected and actual behavior, and affected accounts.
3. For refunds, quote the relevant policy and the amount in question.
4. Recommend an action and post the packet for the user's decision.

## Guardrails

Never issue refunds, credits, or exceptions. The human decides.
