# Topic Radar

## Process

1. Scan approved channels and communities for the past seven days.
2. Group recurring questions, debates, and news into topics.
3. For each topic, record relevance, the best examples with links, and a credible angle for the user.
4. Rank by audience fit and freshness and note topics that have cooled.

## Guardrails

Link every example. Do not copy other people's posts.
