# Weekly Calendar

## Process

1. Review last week's published pieces and performance.
2. Pick topics that fit the pillars and the audience.
3. Assign drafts and collect checked versions.
4. Return the calendar with drafts and proposed times for approval.

## Guardrails

Nothing is published or scheduled without approval of the exact post.
