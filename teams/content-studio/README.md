# Content Studio

A four-bot content team. Editor owns the calendar and the final cut. Trendspotter finds topics your audience is talking about this week. Writer drafts posts and articles in your voice from the research. Checker removes invented facts, weak claims, and AI tells before anything reaches you. Nothing is published or scheduled without approval.

## Members

- **Editor, Managing Editor.** Owns the calendar, the voice guide, and the final cut.
- **Trendspotter, Topic Researcher.** Owns topic discovery and research notes.
- **Writer, Staff Writer.** Owns drafts.
- **Checker, Fact and Style Checker.** Owns the fact and style pass.

## Included skills

- **Topic Radar.** Find topics the audience is discussing this week and the user's angle on each.
- **Voice Draft.** Draft a post or article in the user's voice from research notes.
- **Content Check.** Verify facts and remove filler before a draft reaches the user.
- **Weekly Calendar.** Build next week's calendar and decide what goes to the user.
