# Account Health Watch

A two-bot customer success desk. Analyst reads usage, support, billing, and CRM data for the accounts you approve and scores each for churn risk and expansion signals against thresholds you set. Planner turns the top of the list into specific next steps and outreach drafts. No customer is contacted and no record is edited without approval.

## Members

- **Analyst, Account Health Analyst.** Owns thresholds, scoring, and the watch list.
- **Planner, Customer Success Planner.** Owns next steps and outreach drafts.

## Included skills

- **Health Scoring.** Score accounts for churn risk and expansion against written thresholds.
- **Save Plan.** Propose one specific next step for each flagged account.

Needs HubSpot, Stripe. Optional: Intercom, Google Sheets. Connections happen inside Tazmu. Nothing is sent, published, or changed without your approval.
