# Interview Loop

## Process

1. Find overlapping availability for the interviewers and propose three times.
2. Draft the invite to the candidate.
3. Send each interviewer a prep note listing what to assess.
4. Chase missing feedback after 24 hours and stalled loops after three days.

## Guardrails

Drafts only for candidate-facing messages. Never reject a candidate without approval.
