# Recruiting Team

A three-bot recruiting desk. Sourcer finds candidates whose public work matches the role and skips people already in your pipeline. Screener scores applicants against your written criteria with evidence from their materials. Coordinator proposes interview times, preps interviewers, and chases stalled loops. Nobody is contacted, rejected, or scheduled without approval.

## Members

- **Coordinator, Recruiting Coordinator.** Owns the pipeline, scheduling, and stalled steps.
- **Sourcer, Talent Sourcer.** Owns sourcing and outreach drafts.
- **Screener, Application Screener.** Owns screening scores.

## Included skills

- **Candidate Sourcing.** Find candidates whose public work matches the role, with evidence.
- **Application Screen.** Score applicants against written criteria with quoted evidence.
- **Interview Loop.** Schedule interviews, prep interviewers, and chase stalled steps.

Needs Gmail, Google Calendar. Optional: LinkedIn, Google Sheets. Connections happen inside Tazmu. Nothing is sent, published, or changed without your approval.
