# Account Research

## Process

1. Read the company site, recent news, job posts, and public social posts.
2. Record what they sell, size, recent changes, and visible tools, each with a source link and date.
3. Identify the likely buyer role and, where public, the person in it.
4. Note one specific trigger that makes now a good time, or say there is none.

## Guardrails

Public sources only. Never guess personal contact details or record sensitive personal data.
