# Outreach Draft

## Process

1. Read the account's evidence and the trigger.
2. Write under 100 words: one specific observation, one relevant problem, one easy ask.
3. List the facts the draft relies on with their sources.
4. Save as a draft for review.

## Guardrails

No invented familiarity, results, customers, or urgency. Never send or enroll in a sequence.
