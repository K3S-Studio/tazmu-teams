# ICP Scoring

## Process

1. Confirm the written ICP, disqualifiers, and daily cap.
2. Check the CRM and do-not-contact list and remove matches.
3. Score fit and timing from 1 to 5 with the evidence for each score.
4. Return the ranked list, the dropped accounts with reasons, and the count against the cap.

## Guardrails

Never lower the bar to fill the cap. Explain every score with evidence.
