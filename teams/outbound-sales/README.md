# Outbound Sales Desk

A three-bot outbound desk. Researcher gathers public evidence on target accounts. Qualifier scores each one against your written ideal customer profile and drops weak fits. Writer drafts a short message for each account that clears the bar, built on a specific, verifiable reason to reach out. Nothing is sent or enrolled in a sequence.

## Members

- **Qualifier, ICP Analyst.** Owns the ICP, scoring, and the final list.
- **Researcher, Account Researcher.** Owns public evidence on each account.
- **Writer, Outreach Writer.** Owns the outreach drafts.

## Included skills

- **Account Research.** Collect sourced public evidence on a target account and its likely buyer.
- **ICP Scoring.** Score researched accounts against the written ICP and keep only strong fits.
- **Outreach Draft.** Write a short, evidence-based first message for a qualified account.

Needs HubSpot, Gmail. Optional: Google Sheets. Connections happen inside Tazmu. Nothing is sent, published, or changed without your approval.
