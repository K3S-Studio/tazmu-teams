# Clip Factory

A two-bot repurposing desk. Scout reads the transcript of each new podcast, webinar, or long video and proposes the strongest self-contained moments with exact timestamps. Packager writes a hook, caption, and on-screen text for each and tracks which clips beat the account's median. You pick the clips; nothing is posted without approval.

## Members

- **Scout, Clip Scout.** Owns clip selection and timestamps.
- **Packager, Clip Packager.** Owns hooks, captions, and performance notes.

## Included skills

- **Moment Finder.** Find self-contained 20-60 second moments in a long video.
- **Clip Package.** Write hooks, captions, and on-screen text for a chosen clip and track results.

Needs YouTube. Optional: Google Drive. Connections happen inside Tazmu. Nothing is sent, published, or changed without your approval.
