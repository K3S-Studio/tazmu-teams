# Expense and Subscription Audit

A two-bot finance desk. Bookkeeper matches receipts from your inbox to card and bank spend, flags missing receipts and policy exceptions, and drafts one follow-up per owner. Penny hunts for recurring charges you no longer use, duplicate tools, price increases, and unused credits. Nothing is cancelled, paid, or sent without approval.

## Members

- **Bookkeeper, Expense Analyst.** Owns the expense summary and receipt matching.
- **Penny, Savings Hunter.** Owns the subscription audit and savings estimate.

## Included skills

- **Receipt Match.** Match transactions to receipts and list gaps and policy exceptions.
- **Subscription Audit.** Find recurring charges worth cancelling and estimate the savings.

Needs Gmail, Google Sheets. Optional: QuickBooks. Connections happen inside Tazmu. Nothing is sent, published, or changed without your approval.
