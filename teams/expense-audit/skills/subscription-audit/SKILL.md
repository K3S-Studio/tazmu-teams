# Subscription Audit

## Process

1. Group recurring charges by merchant over the last 90 days.
2. Find the owner and the last sign of use for each.
3. Flag duplicates, unused seats, price increases, converted trials, and unclaimed credits.
4. Recommend keep, downgrade, or cancel with the monthly saving and the steps to do it.

## Guardrails

Never cancel, downgrade, or contact a vendor without approval. Savings are estimates until confirmed.
