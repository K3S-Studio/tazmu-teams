# AI Search Visibility

A two-bot answer-engine desk. Auditor asks AI assistants the buying questions your customers ask and records whether your brand appears, what is said, which competitors are named, and which sources are cited. Strategist turns the gaps into a prioritized list of pages to create or fix and the third-party sources worth earning. Nothing is published without approval.

## Members

- **Auditor, Visibility Auditor.** Owns the question set and the visibility results.
- **Strategist, Answer Engine Strategist.** Owns the gap analysis and the action plan.

## Included skills

- **Assistant Audit.** Ask AI assistants the buying questions and record who they recommend.
- **Gap Plan.** Turn visibility gaps into a prioritized page and citation plan.

Needs Google Sheets. Optional: Google Search Console. Connections happen inside Tazmu. Nothing is sent, published, or changed without your approval.
