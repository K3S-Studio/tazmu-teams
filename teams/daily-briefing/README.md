# Daily Briefing

A two-bot chief-of-staff desk. Scout sweeps the inbox, calendar, and team chat you approve. Chief filters that sweep against your written priorities and returns a short briefing with today's meetings, decisions waiting on you, and anything that changed overnight. Every item links to its source.

## Members

- **Chief, Chief of Staff.** Owns the user's priority list, the cut, and the final briefing.
- **Scout, Inbox and Calendar Analyst.** Owns the raw sweep across inbox, calendar, and chat.

## Included skills

- **Morning Sweep.** Collect every candidate item since the last briefing with its source and a reason it might matter.
- **Priority Filter.** Cut the sweep down to the few items that need the user today.

Needs Gmail, Google Calendar. Optional: Slack. Connections happen inside Tazmu. Nothing is sent, published, or changed without your approval.
