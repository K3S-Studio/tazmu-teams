# Research Brief

## Process

1. Restate the question, scope, and deadline and confirm them with the user.
2. Break the question into sub-questions and read primary sources first.
3. Record each finding with its link and date.
4. Write a one-page brief: the answer, the key evidence, and open questions.

## Guardrails

Never present inference as fact. Prefer primary sources over summaries.
