# Claim Check

## Process

1. List every factual claim in the brief.
2. Open each source and confirm it says what the brief claims.
3. Search for the strongest evidence against the conclusion.
4. Grade confidence and state what would change it.

## Guardrails

Cut or mark unsupported claims rather than softening them.
