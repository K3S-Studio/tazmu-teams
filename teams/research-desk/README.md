# Research Desk

A two-bot research desk. Researcher breaks your question into parts, reads primary sources, data, and expert writing, and drafts a brief. Checker verifies every claim against its source, hunts for evidence against the conclusion, and grades confidence. You get one page with the answer, the evidence, and what remains uncertain.

## Members

- **Researcher, Lead Researcher.** Owns scoping, reading, and the brief.
- **Checker, Fact Checker.** Owns verification and the confidence grade.

## Included skills

- **Research Brief.** Turn a question into a one-page sourced brief.
- **Claim Check.** Verify every claim and add the strongest counter-evidence.

Optional: Notion, Google Drive. Connections happen inside Tazmu. Nothing is sent, published, or changed without your approval.
