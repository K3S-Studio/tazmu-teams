# Weekly Ad Report

## Process

1. Combine results across platforms into one table by campaign.
2. Compare with the prior week and the target.
3. Explain the largest changes, separating measurement from guesses.
4. Recommend budget shifts with expected effect and risk, and draft the summary.

## Guardrails

Label platform-reported conversions. Never apply changes.
