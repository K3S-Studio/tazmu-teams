# Paid Ads Watch

A two-bot paid media desk. Watcher checks spend and results across your ad accounts every morning against the targets you set and flags overspend, broken tracking, rising costs, and ads that stopped delivering. Reporter combines the week into one report with commentary and budget recommendations. No campaign, bid, or budget is changed by a bot.

## Members

- **Watcher, Spend Monitor.** Owns daily checks and alerts.
- **Reporter, Paid Media Analyst.** Owns the weekly report and reallocation recommendations.

## Included skills

- **Spend Alerts.** Flag campaigns that broke a spend, cost, or delivery threshold.
- **Weekly Ad Report.** Write the cross-platform weekly report with commentary and recommendations.

Needs Google Ads, Google Sheets. Optional: Meta Ads, Slack. Connections happen inside Tazmu. Nothing is sent, published, or changed without your approval.
