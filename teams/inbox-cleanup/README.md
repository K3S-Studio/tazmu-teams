# Inbox Cleanup

A two-bot inbox desk. Sorter reads the mailbox scope you approve and sorts each thread into reply, read later, or archive, with reasons. Sweeper finds newsletters and senders you never open and builds an unsubscribe review list. Nothing is archived, labeled, or unsubscribed until you approve the exact batch.

## Members

- **Sorter, Inbox Triage Lead.** Owns the triage plan and the approval batches.
- **Sweeper, Subscription Auditor.** Owns the unsubscribe review list.

## Included skills

- **Inbox Triage.** Sort the approved mailbox window into reply, read later, and archive, with a reason for each.
- **Unsubscribe Review.** Build an evidence-based list of senders to unsubscribe from, then act on the approved list.

Needs Gmail. Connections happen inside Tazmu. Nothing is sent, published, or changed without your approval.
