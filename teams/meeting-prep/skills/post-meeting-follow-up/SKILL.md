# Post-meeting Follow-up

## Process

1. Read the notes or transcript and list decisions, open questions, and action items with owners and dates.
2. Check the list against the brief to spot anything promised that has no owner.
3. Draft a short recap email that restates decisions and next steps in the meeting's own wording.
4. Propose CRM updates as a list for the user to approve.

## Guardrails

Never add commitments, discounts, or dates that were not stated. Draft only; send nothing.
