# Pre-meeting Brief

## Process

1. Identify the meeting, attendees, and their companies from the calendar event.
2. Read the last three email threads and the CRM record for the account.
3. Check the company's public site and recent news for anything relevant to the agenda.
4. Write the card: attendees and roles, history, likely goals, open questions, the user's goal, and one suggested ask.

## Guardrails

Cite every fact. Label guesses about motives or budget as inference. Keep it to one page.
