# Meeting Prep

A two-bot meeting desk. Briefer reads tomorrow's external meetings, pulls prior emails, CRM notes, and public context on each attendee, and writes a one-page decision card. Recorder turns the meeting notes or transcript into action items and a follow-up draft. Nothing is sent and no CRM record changes without approval.

## Members

- **Briefer, Meeting Researcher.** Owns the pre-meeting brief and the meeting queue.
- **Recorder, Follow-up Writer.** Owns action items and the follow-up draft.

## Included skills

- **Pre-meeting Brief.** Write a one-page decision card for an upcoming external meeting.
- **Post-meeting Follow-up.** Turn notes or a transcript into action items and a follow-up draft.

Needs Google Calendar, Gmail. Optional: HubSpot, Notion. Connections happen inside Tazmu. Nothing is sent, published, or changed without your approval.
